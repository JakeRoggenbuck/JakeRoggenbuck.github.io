## Introduction

I had the crazy idea of attaching a vintage Exakta lens to my modern Sony a6400.

<img src="../../images/rotated_img_2398_720.avif" alt="Vintage Exakta lens attached to a Sony a6400 camera" title="Exakta lens on a Sony a6400" height="720">

I first wanted to see how I could non-permanently yet securely attach something to the body of my camera. I figured there must be some sort of camera body cover, and sure enough I found a two pack on Amazon for $7. Sweet!

Before starting, I wanted to figure out spacing of the lens. I held the lens up to the camera without attaching it, and I found that the lens needed to be a centimeter or two away from the camera body. This meant I would need to make a spacer somehow.

With the camera body cover delivered, I got to work.

### 1. Cut a circle spacer out of wood

I used a jigsaw to cut a circle out some scrap pine wood to act as a spacer.

The circle was drawn using the camera body cover as a template.

<img src="../../images/img_2375.jpg.avif" alt="Drawing the circle spacer on scrap pine wood using the camera body cover as a template" title="Tracing the spacer circle" height="720">

### 2. Find the center

I found the center of the circle by drawing many lines.

<img src="../../images/img_2374.jpg.avif" alt="Finding the center of the wood circle by drawing many lines" title="Finding the center of the spacer" height="720">

### 3. Drill out a hole to let light through

I drilled a hole in the spacer with a 1.5 inch paddle bit...

<img src="../../images/img_2376.jpg.avif" alt="Drilling a 1.5 inch hole in the wood spacer with a paddle bit" title="Drilling the spacer hole" height="720">

And snap! It broke.

<img src="../../images/img_2377.jpg.avif" alt="Wood spacer broken from drilling without a pilot hole" title="Broken wood spacer" height="720">

### 4. Drill a hole in the scrap wood first

I learned from my mistake and started by drilling a 1.5 inch hole with the same bit in the scrap wood first.

I first found the center the same way.

<img src="../../images/img_2378.jpg.avif" alt="Finding the center of a new piece of scrap wood" title="Centering the next spacer attempt" height="720">

And then I drilled the hole.

<img src="../../images/img_2379_720.jpg.avif" alt="Drilling the 1.5 inch hole in the scrap wood before cutting out the spacer" title="Drilling the hole before cutting" height="720">

### 5. Cut out the spacer

I cut out the spacer with the hole already drilled.

<img src="../../images/img_2380.jpg.avif" alt="Cut out wood spacer with the hole already drilled" title="Spacer cut out" height="720">

I also sanded down the spacer with two grits of sandpaper.

<img src="../../images/img_2381_720.jpg.avif" alt="Sanded wood spacer ready to attach to the lens" title="Sanded wood spacer" height="720">

### 6. Glue the space to the lens

I used hot glue to glue the spacer to the lens of the Exakta.

<img src="../../images/img_2388.jpg.avif" alt="Hot gluing the wood spacer to the Exakta lens" title="Gluing the spacer to the lens" height="720">

<img src="../../images/img_2389_720.jpg.avif" alt="Wood spacer glued to the Exakta lens" title="Spacer attached to the lens" height="720">

### 7. Find the center of the cover

I found the center of the camera body cover using the many lines trick.

<img src="../../images/img_2383_720.jpg.avif" alt="Finding the center of the camera body cover using the many-lines trick" title="Centering the camera body cover" height="720">

### 8. Drill a pilot hole

I drilled a pilot hole with a small bit to make the centering of the paddle bit easier.

<img src="../../images/img_2384_720.jpg.avif" alt="Drilling a small pilot hole in the camera body cover" title="Pilot hole in the camera body cover" height="720">

### 9. Drill a hole with a 1 inch paddle bit

I drilled a hole in the camera body cover with a 1 inch paddle bit to allow light through.

<img src="../../images/img_2395_720.jpg.avif" alt="One inch hole drilled through the camera body cover to let light through" title="Camera body cover with light hole" height="720">

And sanded down the edges with two different grits of sandpaper.

<img src="../../images/img_2387.jpg.avif" alt="Camera body cover after sanding the drilled edges" title="Sanded camera body cover" height="720">

I also washed the piece in the sink and dried it off with a towel.

### 10. I glued the cover to the spacer

I used hot glue to attach the spacer to the camera body cover to complete the build.

<img src="../../images/img_2396_720.jpg.avif" alt="Hot gluing the spacer to the camera body cover to complete the build" title="Gluing the spacer to the camera body cover" height="720">
